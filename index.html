How these numbers are made (and how good they are)

Every projection comes from a state-space (Kalman) engine fit on play-by-play-era box scores — the same family of model as the best public systems. Per-game numbers are talent rate × a separately-modeled minutes forecast; the ± is the filter's own uncertainty, and the games range is a fitted availability distribution, not a guess. The style tag names the archetype whose statistical shape the player most resembles.

Honesty first: on held-out seasons this board beats naive baselines on every stat, roughly ties the fantasy market's consensus ranking, and its accuracy claims are reproducible from versioned code. A public track record against other systems starts with the 2026-27 season and will be posted here, win or lose.
